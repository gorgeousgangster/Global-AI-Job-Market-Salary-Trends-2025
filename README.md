## Project Objective
This comprehensive dataset contains detailed information about AI and machine learning job positions, salaries, and market trends across different countries, experience levels, and company sizes. Perfect for data science enthusiasts, career researchers, and market analysts for practice purposes. To support this, I have designed and developed a dynamic and interactive AI Job Market and Salary Trend Dashboard using Power BI.

## Dataset used
- <a href = "https://github.com/gorgeousgangster/Global-AI-Job-Market-Salary-Trends-2025/blob/main/ai_job_dataset%20(1).csv"> Dataset</a>

## Question
- Market Overview
  + Is the AI job market growing or declining?
  + Is remote work a dominant trend?
  + Which regions have the highest demand?
- Skill Demand
  + Which skills are most in demand?
  + Which skill groups matter most (Programming, Cloud, ML)?
  + Which skills lead to higher salaries?
- Company & Hiring
  + Which company size hires the most?
  + How does company size affect remote work?
  + What skills do companies prioritize?
- Industry & Jobs
  + Which industries hire the most?
  + Which job categories pay the highest?
  + Is AI demand concentrated in tech or across industries?
- Experience & Salary
  + Which experience level is most in demand?
  + How does salary grow with experience?
  + What is the gap between entry-level and senior salaries?
- Remote Work
  + Which roles offer the most remote opportunities?
  + Does remote work affect salary?
- Key Insight Questions
  + What drives demand: skills, location, or company size?
  + Which skills provide the highest ROI (salary growth)?
  + Is the market favoring junior or senior talent?
